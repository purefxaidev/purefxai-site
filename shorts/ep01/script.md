## Season 1 outline (5 episodes)
1. **EP1: The loser and the golden window.** Tham goes broke and loses his job, is transported to another world, receives the PureFxAi System and survives his first goblin through analysis.
2. **EP2: The market town of chance.** In a trading town where everyone gambles on gut feeling, Tham uses the system to analyze prices and trends. He builds his first capital properly and meets Mira (มีรา), a sharp-tongued merchant. Outfit: dark leather armor with gold lines.
3. **EP3: Discipline over desire.** He nearly loses everything again through greed and learns risk management (setting a stop loss). He fights a forest beast by planning, not brute force. The system unlocks the "AI Tutor" skill.
4. **EP4: The hooded figure.** The watcher reveals himself: another user of a corrupted "Black System" that only cares about short-term profit. Tham beats him with patience and data. Outfit: black suit-style armor with the gold PureFxAi emblem.
5. **EP5: God tier.** Tham reaches the highest level. He creates "Automation" magic and begins teaching the townspeople to think with data. The ending: he chooses to return to Earth to teach the people who used to be like him. Final hook: the PureFxAi System glows in his room on Earth.
