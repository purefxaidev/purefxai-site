# เพียว จอมยุทธ์ไร้กระบี่ (Pure: The Swordless Wanderer)

Genre: กำลังภายใน / Chinese wuxia (action-comedy, warm heart) · Vertical 9:16 · ~2 min per episode (8 scenes x 15 s, each scene = 4 timed shots) · Thai dialogue (lip-synced) · PG-13 (stylised, bloodless action)
All characters are fictional. Original faces only; no real person, celebrity, actor or the owner. Lock every face and costume with one reference image per character and reuse it in every scene.

## Logline
เพียว เด็กล้างจานโรงเตี๊ยมริมแม่น้ำที่ไม่เคยถือกระบี่ บังเอิญได้ฟังเคล็ดวิชา "ลมหายใจสายน้ำ" จากชายชราขี้เมา เขาจึงสู้ด้วยตะเกียบ ไม้กวาด และกาน้ำชา จนยุทธภพทั้งแผ่นดินต้องจับตามอง
Pure, a dishwasher at a riverside inn who has never held a sword, overhears the "River Breath" technique from a drunk old man. Armed with chopsticks, brooms and teapots, he becomes the hero the martial world never saw coming.

## Characters (reference-locked, fictional)
- เพียว (Pure), 22 - hero. Lean build, sun-tanned skin, messy black topknot tied with a frayed red cord, faded indigo cotton robe with rolled sleeves, a patched apron, wooden chopsticks tucked behind his ear. Kind, quick-witted, terrible at bowing properly. Reference: `refs/wuxia-pure.png`.
- ซูหลิน (Su Lin), 21 - swordswoman of the Jade Lotus sect. Long hair in a half-up bun with a single jade hairpin, pale green layered hanfu with white inner collar, slim sword with a green tassel. Serious, secretly finds Pure funny. Reference: `refs/wuxia-sulin.png`.
- ตาเฒ่าเหล้าไห (Old Jar), ~70 - hidden master pretending to be a drunk. Wild grey beard, straw hat, patched brown robe, a clay wine jar on a rope. Reference: `refs/wuxia-oldjar.png`.
- เถ้าแก่หม่า (Boss Ma), 50 - inn owner, stingy and loud, round belly, maroon vest, abacus always in hand. Comic relief. Reference: `refs/wuxia-ma.png`.
- หัวหน้าหน้ากากเหล็ก (Iron Mask), 40s - villain enforcer of the Black Cloud gang; black lamellar armour, half iron mask, heavy saber. Reference: `refs/wuxia-ironmask.png`.

## Look (every scene)
Photorealistic 35mm handheld film look, natural and soft, NOT sharp or over-detailed: gentle film grain, slight motion blur on fast moves, soft focus fall-off, natural skin texture, muted earthy colours (indigo, jade, rust, warm lantern amber), real daylight or lantern light. Misty river town, wooden inns, bamboo forest, stone bridges. No on-screen text, no logos, no subtitles burned in (subtitles are added later). Action is graceful and bloodless; comedy comes from timing and reactions.

## Season outline
1. ตะเกียบคู่นี้ไม่ได้มีไว้กินข้าว - Pure defends the inn from Black Cloud thugs with chopsticks, after overhearing Old Jar mumble a technique.
2. ศิษย์ที่ไม่มีใครรับ - Pure begs Old Jar to teach him; training is "chores" (carrying water, sweeping leaves) until Su Lin tests him.
3. สะพานหิน ยามหมอก - Iron Mask comes for the inn's debt; Pure, Su Lin and a teapot face him on the stone bridge.
4. (next) ความลับของไหเหล้า - who Old Jar really is.
5. (next) กระบี่เล่มแรก - Pure finally holds a sword, and finds he fights better without it.
