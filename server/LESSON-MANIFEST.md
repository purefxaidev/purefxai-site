## Script outlines (1 paragraph each)
- **ai-basics/l1:** The teacher introduces AI as computer systems that do tasks needing human-like intelligence. She gives everyday examples (YouTube recommendations, voice assistants, photo face detection) and contrasts them with non-AI tools like a calculator. She ends with: AI learns patterns, it does not "think" like a human.
- **ai-basics/l2:** Explains machine learning as learning patterns from example data instead of hand-written rules. Uses a simple analogy: showing many cat and dog photos until the model can tell them apart. Stresses "garbage in, garbage out": data quality decides result quality.
- **ai-basics/l3:** Safe, effective ChatGPT use. Always verify facts because AI can hallucinate. Never paste passwords, ID numbers or private customer data. Use AI to draft, summarize, translate and brainstorm, while the human makes the final decision.
- **prompt/l1:** The four parts of a strong prompt: role, task, context and output format. The teacher rewrites a weak prompt ("write an article") into a strong one step by step and shows the difference in results on screen.
- **prompt/l2:** Iteration. When an answer misses, add constraints, give an example of the desired output and ask the AI to ask clarifying questions. Demonstrates three rounds of refinement on one task.
- **automation/l1:** What a workflow is: trigger, steps, output. Example: a form is submitted, AI summarizes it, and a summary email is sent to the team. Introduces no-code tools in general terms and where AI fits in the chain.
- **vibe-coding/l1:** Vibe coding: describe the website or app you want to an AI coding tool (e.g. Cursor or ChatGPT), review the code, test, ask for fixes one at a time, then deploy. Shows a tiny landing page being built from a prompt, plus the habit of testing after every change.
- **vibe-coding/l2:** Briefing for the hands-on task: build a one-page landing site with a hero, 3 pricing tiers and a signup form using AI. Gives a starter prompt, a checklist (mobile-friendly, working form, clear CTA) and explains how to submit a link or the code.
- **ai-trading/l1:** How AI can help with market analysis: summarizing news, organizing historical data and checking a trading plan for consistency. Clear statement that AI cannot predict markets or guarantee profit, that risk management and stop losses come first, and that the content is educational only, not investment advice.
